## The difference

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal, every
period, and never accelerates. A $10,000 loan at 6% simple interest
costs exactly $600 in interest every year, for as many years as it runs.

Compound interest adds each period's interest back into the principal,
so future interest is calculated on a growing base — see the full
mechanism.

## Why it matters

The gap between the two grows with time and rate. Over a single year
the difference is often negligible; over decades, or at high interest
rates like credit card debt, the difference becomes the entire story.
