# Phase 3 Topics — Subtopic Maps

_Parent topics: Personal Finance Basics, Legal & Documentation How-Tos, Technology Basics, Career & Study Skills, Home & DIY Knowledge (all Phase 3 — Adult Practical Life Skills)_

Each topic below has 50 subtopics grouped into 5–8 clusters. Each subtopic maps to one or more entries using `entry-template.md`. Subtopics are deliberately scoped narrow — one post, one genuinely complete idea (theory + a practical, relatable angle) — rather than broad umbrella topics that force a shallow highlight-reel treatment. `personal-finance-basics` and `legal-documentation-howtos` are YMYL: every subtopic below is scoped as factual/how-the-system-works content (how a mechanism works, what a term means, what a process requires), not personalized financial or legal advice — see Section 5 and 9 of `site-rules.md`.
