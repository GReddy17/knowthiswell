# Phase 2 Topics — Subtopic Maps

_Parent topics: Units & Measurement Conversions, General Awareness & Current Affairs Basics, Health & Body Basics, Environment & Nature (all Phase 2)_
_Last updated: 2026-08-19_

Each topic below has 50 subtopics grouped into 5–6 clusters. Each subtopic maps to one or more entries using `entry-template.md`. Scoping follows the lesson learned in Phase 1: subtopics are kept narrow enough for one entry to give genuinely complete coverage (theory + a practical, relatable angle), not broad umbrella topics that force a shallow highlight-reel treatment.

Overlap check: `health-body-basics` is scoped as body maintenance/hygiene/prevention/first-aid/health-literacy, deliberately distinct from Phase 1 General Science Facts' Biology & Human Body cluster (which already owns organ-system mechanism content — how digestion, circulation, hormones, etc. actually work). `environment-nature` is scoped as human-environment interaction, pollution, conservation action, sustainability, and policy — deliberately distinct from Phase 1's Earth Science and Life Science/Nature clusters (which already own the foundational science of ecosystems, weather, geology, and species).

## Notes on usage

- This file covers all 4 Phase 2 topics, continuing the same clustering and scoping logic as `phase1-subtopics.md`.
- Every subtopic was checked against Phase 1's already-published clusters (see `phase1-subtopics.md`) to avoid duplicate coverage — `health-body-basics` and `environment-nature` in particular sit deliberately on the applied/preventive/policy side of their subjects, not the foundational-science side already owned by General Science Facts.
- `health-body-basics` carries `ymyl: true` in `content/taxonomy.json` — every entry drafted from this cluster must follow Section 5/9 of `site-rules.md` (informational framing, real sources, no direct medical advice).
- `general-awareness-basics` subtopics were deliberately scoped as evergreen civic/institutional literacy, not a running news log, since time-sensitive events age out and duplicate what a search engine already does better than a reference site can.
