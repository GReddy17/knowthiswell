_Parent topics: Investing & Markets Deep Dive, Government Schemes & Benefits, Competitive Exam Prep, Professional Skills & Certifications, AI & Future Tech Literacy, Digital Safety & Privacy, Life Skills & Etiquette (all Phase 5, per `content/taxonomy.json`)_
_Last updated: 2026-08-19_

Each topic below has 50 subtopics grouped into 5–10 clusters, following the same format as `phase1-subtopics.md`. Subtopics are scoped narrow-and-complete on purpose — Phase 1 shipped a handful of entries (Human Body Systems, Animal Kingdom Facts, Solar System & Planets) whose titles named more sub-things than one entry could actually explain, and those had to be expanded after the fact. These subtopic titles are written to avoid that from the start: each one should be answerable, in full, by a single entry covering both theory and a practical angle.

Two of these topics (Investing & Markets Deep Dive, Government Schemes & Benefits) are YMYL and inherently time-sensitive — subtopics are scoped as factual/mechanism content ("how X works"), not personalized advice, and both categories will need tighter `lastReviewed` discipline than most once written (a content-ops concern, not something baked into the titles).

Recommend flagging both categories for more frequent `lastReviewed` passes than the rest of the site once written.
- Sensitive-adjacent clusters (funeral/condolence etiquette, elderly scam protection, identity theft, sextortion) need the same factual-and-respectful treatment called out for Phase 1's sensitive clusters — see Section 5 of `site-rules.md`.
