## Open caveat, carried from the demand-check discussion

Google Trends measures typed-search intent — useful for confirming broad
topic appeal (Nobel Prize, light pollution, languages) but a weak signal
for Shorts specifically, since Shorts distribution is algorithmic
For-You-feed discovery, not search-driven. A topic with zero search volume
can still break out on hook strength alone. Treat the Tier S demand
confirmation as a bonus signal on top of hook quality, not a replacement
for it.
