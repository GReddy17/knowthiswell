# Pillar topic queue — counterintuitive everyday science

The daily pillar post pulls the next unused idea from here. Each is a wrong
intuition most people hold, plus the real mechanism. Mark `[x]` when written and
add the slug. Keep this list topped up (aim for 20+ unused at all times).

Format: `- [ ] Wrong intuition → real mechanism  (subtopic)`

## Physical world

- [ ] Heavier objects fall faster → in a vacuum a feather and a hammer land together; air resistance is the whole story  (forces-and-motion)
- [ ] You see lightning before you hear thunder because it "happens first" → they happen together; light is ~900,000× faster than sound  (light-color-and-sound)
- [ ] Cold flows into a room → there is no "cold"; heat flows out, and you feel the loss  (heat-and-temperature)
- [ ] A microwave heats from the inside out → it heats water molecules everywhere the waves reach, ~1 cm deep; the middle is last  (electromagnetic-basics)
- [ ] Metal is colder than wood → both are room temperature; metal pulls heat from your hand faster, so it feels colder  (heat-and-temperature)
- [ ] Heavier things sink → density vs. the fluid decides it; a steel ship floats, a steel bolt sinks  (fluids-and-pressure)
- [ ] A bird on a live wire is safe because the wire is insulated → it is bare; the bird is safe because there is no voltage difference across its feet  (electricity-and-magnetism-basics)
- [ ] Thick wires and thin wires carry current equally → thin wires have more resistance, so they heat up and can melt  (electricity-and-magnetism-basics)
- [ ] Water conducts electricity → pure water barely does; the dissolved salts and minerals do  (electricity-and-magnetism-basics)
- [ ] The seasons are caused by Earth's distance from the Sun → Earth is closest to the Sun in January; axial tilt causes seasons  (space-and-astronomy)
- [ ] The Moon has a permanent dark side → it has a permanent *far* side; every part gets sunlight over a month  (space-and-astronomy)
- [ ] Rockets push against the air to move → they push against their own exhaust; they work better in vacuum  (forces-and-motion)
- [ ] Gyroscopes defy gravity → angular momentum resists changes in orientation; gravity still acts fully  (forces-and-motion)
- [ ] Hot water freezes slower than cold → sometimes it freezes faster (the Mpemba effect); evaporation and convection  (heat-and-temperature)
- [ ] Glass is a slow-flowing liquid → it is an amorphous solid; old windows are thicker at the bottom from how they were made  (matter-and-states-of-matter)
- [ ] Adding salt makes water boil faster → it raises the boiling point slightly; the water is hotter, food cooks a touch faster  (matter-and-states-of-matter)
- [x] Lightning never strikes the same place twice → tall structures get hit repeatedly; the Empire State Building ~25×/year  (light-color-and-sound) (written: general-science-facts/does-lightning-really-never-strike-the-same-place-twice)
- [ ] A falling cat lands on its feet by "twisting off" something → it uses conservation of angular momentum, no external push needed  (forces-and-motion)

## Body & biology

- [ ] We use only 10% of our brains → nearly all of it is active over a day; damage anywhere causes deficits  (biology-and-human-body)
- [ ] Sugar makes kids hyperactive → controlled trials show no effect; expectation bias in parents  (nutrition-and-diet-basics)
- [ ] Cracking knuckles causes arthritis → it is gas bubbles collapsing; long-term studies show no link  (biology-and-human-body)
- [ ] Shaving makes hair grow back thicker → the blunt cut tip *feels* coarser; growth rate and thickness are unchanged  (biology-and-human-body)
- [ ] You lose most body heat through your head → proportional to exposed surface area; the head is just often uncovered  (biology-and-human-body)
- [ ] Reading in dim light damages your eyes → it causes temporary strain, not lasting damage  (biology-and-human-body)
- [ ] The tongue has separate zones for each taste → all taste buds detect all five tastes; the "tongue map" is a mistranslation  (biology-and-human-body)
- [ ] Blood is blue inside your body → it is always red; veins look blue through skin because of how light scatters  (biology-and-human-body)
- [ ] Goldfish have a 3-second memory → they can be trained and remember for months  (life-science-and-nature)
- [ ] Bats are blind → most see fine; echolocation is an addition, not a replacement  (life-science-and-nature)
