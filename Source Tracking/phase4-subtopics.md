# Phase 4 Topics — Subtopic Maps

_Parent topics: Health & Wellness Deep Dive, Technology Deep Dives, Business & Entrepreneurship Basics, Travel & Local Guides, Trivia & Fun Facts (all Phase 4)_
_Last updated: 2026-08-19_

Each topic below has 50 subtopics grouped into 5–6 clusters. Each subtopic maps to one or more entries using `entry-template.md`. Subtopics here are deliberately scoped narrow — one post, one concept, both theory and a practical/relatable angle — following the lesson learned expanding Phase 1: broad multi-part subtopics (e.g. "Human Body Systems," "Solar System & Planets") shipped as shallow highlight reels until they were revisited and expanded; narrow subtopics (e.g. "Acids, Bases & pH," "Density & Buoyancy") got full coverage on the first pass. This file plans for full coverage from the start.

## Notes on usage

- Follows the same clustering/priority-order conventions as `phase1-subtopics.md`. Read that file first before drafting entries from this one.
- `Health & Wellness Deep Dive` is YMYL — every entry needs real institutional sourcing (NIH, CDC, Mayo Clinic, peer-reviewed summaries) and must avoid diagnostic/prescriptive language per `site-rules.md` Section 5/9.
- `Technology Deep Dives` and `Health & Wellness Deep Dive` are explicitly framed as "one level deeper" than their Phase 2/3 counterparts (`Technology Basics`, `Health & Body Basics`) — cross-link liberally via `seeAlso` once both phases are written.
- `Trivia & Fun Facts` clusters are thematic, not hierarchical — it's fine for entries in this topic to be shorter/lighter than other topics as long as each one is still a complete, standalone entry rather than a bare list.
