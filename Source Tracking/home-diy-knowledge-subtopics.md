# Home & DIY Knowledge — Subtopic Map (Category 15)

_Parent topic: Home & DIY Knowledge (Phase 3, general-audience, non-locale-specific)_
_Last updated: 2026-09-03_
_Author: James H. Rivers_

Deliberately scoped as *mechanism-level* home maintenance and repair knowledge —
"how does X actually work / why does X happen / what's actually different
between X and Y," not region-specific building-code walkthroughs or step-by-step
installation manuals. No named current brands, tool models, or prices used as
running examples — every mechanism is described generically so it doesn't date.
Electrical, structural, and gas-adjacent topics are YMYL-adjacent: state facts,
cite real safety-standards bodies, and point to a licensed professional for
anything beyond general knowledge (never give specific personal how-to
instructions for hazardous work).

50 posts across 10 subtopic clusters of 5 posts each. `order` is continuous
1-50 across the whole category.
